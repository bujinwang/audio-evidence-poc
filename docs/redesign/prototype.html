What this buys

At 100 hours of new 911 audio a day, a full index costs about $36 a day and buys nothing until someone searches it. Windowed promotion costs $0.24 per tape and answers the reopened case in four minutes.

Where it breaks

  • Spotting is a prototype: recall measured on 40 tapes, not 3,900.
  • Overlapping speech still defeats the windowing heuristic.
  • Above 1M segments, SQLite FTS gives way to Postgres; p95 was not measured.
  • Denoising is skipped on triage — it costs more than transcription.
